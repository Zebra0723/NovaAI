# GalaxyAI ideas backlog

## Confirmed installments
1. CometAI: iMessage agent
2. OrbitAI: voice agent (only speaks when spoken to)
3. NovaAI: on-screen agent
4. ZenithAI: the commander of the Galaxy. See ZENITH_PLAN.md
5. NebulaAI: the Galaxy's hands, gesture control on the Mac. See NEBULA_PLAN.md

The senses: Comet = text, Orbit = voice, Nova = sight, Nebula = touch (gesture), Zenith = time

## Installment 6 candidates (not chosen yet)
- **Pulsar (hearing)**: hears what the Mac hears, only while switched on. Live captions, searchable transcripts, translation and key points, with speech-to-text running on the Mac itself
- **Halo (presence)**: the Galaxy follows you across Mac, iPhone and Watch, with hand-off, Live Activities and widgets
- **Lyra (taste)**: learns your taste in everything, picks for you, and teaches it to the other members
- **Quasar (reach)**: works inside your online accounts (Gmail, Drive, Spotify, Discord…) even with the Mac asleep

## Galaxy Tools (saved for later, not installments)
- **Galaxy Web**: the whole Galaxy from any browser on any computer
- **Galaxy Search**: one search bar for the web, your files, mail and memory; answers, not links, and it can act
- **Galaxy Mail**: an AI with its own email address; forward or CC it anything
