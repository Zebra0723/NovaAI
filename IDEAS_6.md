# Installment 6: fifty general ideas

The rule for every idea here: it works for **any task and any person**. It isn't built around a subject (school, tennis, money, health) or a single job. What makes each one new is **how** the AI works for you, not **what** it works on.

The Galaxy today:

| Member | What's new about how it works |
|---|---|
| Comet | You text it |
| Orbit | You talk to it |
| Nova | It uses your screen |
| Nebula | It reads your hands |
| Zenith | It works for hours or days, on its own computer |

The ideas are grouped by what kind of new they are. ★ marks the ten strongest, which are summarised at the end.

---

## A. New ways to reach the whole Galaxy
Each of these is a new door into all five members at once.

1. **★ Halo: presence.** The Galaxy follows you across your Mac, iPhone, iPad, Watch and car. Start something on the Mac and finish it on your phone; approve something on your wrist. One assistant that's wherever you are, not stuck on one device.
2. **Echo: a phone number.** Call your Galaxy from any phone at all: a friend's, a car, a landline. You talk to it like a person and it can use every member. It works when you've no data, no app and no Mac nearby.
3. **★ Io: speak anywhere it should write.** Hold a key and talk in any text box, in any app. You get finished writing: punctuation, formatting and tone that fit where you are (a text, an email, a document, code). The fastest way to put words anywhere.
4. **★ Meteor: one key for the next step.** Press one key anywhere and the Galaxy does the obvious next thing for what's in front of you. Reply to the email, fill in the form, fix the error, finish the sentence, summarise the page. No prompt.
5. **Nimbus: everywhere hooks.** Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, the Share sheet, right-click menus, Spotlight and the Lock Screen all lead into the Galaxy. Wherever Apple lets something plug in, the Galaxy is there.
6. **★ Lumen: lives in the notch.** The MacBook's notch becomes the Galaxy's home, the way the Dynamic Island is on iPhone. It shows live work, quick answers and approvals you can tap. Always visible and never in the way.
7. **Beacon: the Galaxy as a contact.** A real contact card in Messages, Mail, WhatsApp and FaceTime. Add it to any conversation (a group chat with friends, an email thread) and it helps everyone there.
8. **Portal: the Galaxy on any screen.** Open it from any computer's browser, including school and library computers, with your memory, your members and your Mac all reachable. (Close to Galaxy Web from Galaxy Tools.)
9. **Lantern: CarPlay.** The Galaxy in the car: hands-free and road-safe, reading and answering what you need on the way.
10. **Vesta: the Galaxy at home.** A HomePod or old iPad becomes a home station that anyone in the house can talk to or glance at, with your permission.

## B. New ways AI does the work
How the work gets done is what's new, not where you ask for it.

11. **★ Titan: AI at scale.** Give it a thousand of anything (files, photos, emails, rows, links, messages) and one instruction: rename, sort, fix, tag, translate, summarise, extract, reply. It does all thousand and shows you the few it wasn't sure about.
12. **★ Prism: turn anything into anything.** Point at anything and say what it should become: a video into notes, notes into slides, a photo of a table into a spreadsheet, a voice memo into an email, a sketch into a working web page, a long thread into a decision.
13. **Corona: a co-editor in every app.** It works *with* you inside whatever you're making (a doc, slides, code, a design), editing alongside your cursor live, like a second person in the file.
14. **Shadow: learns by watching.** It notices what you do repeatedly, without being asked, and offers to take it over, once, quietly. ("You rename screenshots every day. Want me to do it?")
15. **Gravity: everything you need, pulled in.** Start any task and it gathers every relevant file, message, link, note and past conversation into one place in seconds.
16. **Mirage: a dry run of anything.** Before any action (sending the email, running the command, deleting the folder, publishing the post), it shows exactly what will happen, so you approve with confidence.
17. **Atlas: a map of all your stuff.** It builds a live map of your digital life (projects, people, files, accounts) and keeps it up to date, so any member can find anything instantly.
18. **Chronos: rewind your Mac.** Undo anything: a file you overwrote, a setting you broke, an app you deleted, a tab you closed last week. It's an AI-powered time machine for everything.
19. **★ Relay: the Galaxy on autopilot between apps.** It moves information between your apps for you. Something arrives in Mail and it's in Calendar, Notes and Reminders, filed and linked. You never copy and paste again.
20. **Sentinel: keeps an eye out.** You tell it what to watch for (a price, a reply, a change, an announcement) and it acts when that happens. It never talks; you just find it done.

## C. New senses
A new way of perceiving the world, after text, voice, sight, touch and time.

21. **★ Pulsar: hearing.** It hears what your Mac hears, only when switched on: anything playing, calls, videos. Live captions, searchable transcripts and key points, with speech-to-text running on the Mac itself.
22. **Lyra: taste.** It learns what you like across everything and teaches it to the Galaxy, so everything the members make or pick already looks and feels like you.
23. **Sirius: protection.** The Galaxy's immune system. It catches scams, phishing, bad downloads and leaked passwords before they reach you.
24. **Polaris: guidance.** It teaches you to do anything in any app, with arrows and highlights on your real screen, step by step.
25. **Kepler: discovery.** It brings you what's new about the things you care about, gathered into one feed you open when you want it.
26. **Orion: place.** It knows where you are and what's around, and adapts every member to it: home, school, a friend's house, travelling.
27. **Tempo: rhythm.** It learns how your days actually go and quietly lines the Galaxy up with them: heavy work overnight, answers ready by morning, quiet when you're busy.
28. **Aurora: ambient sight.** A spare screen that shows the Galaxy's living state at a glance: what's running, what's done, what needs you.
29. **Vega: a sense of a project.** It understands every project you're working on as a whole, across files, apps, people and conversations, and keeps each one's state.
30. **Draco: reflex.** Instant, free answers from a small model running on the Mac, for everything that doesn't need the big models. The Galaxy gets faster and cheaper overall.

## D. New kinds of helper
A different relationship with you.

31. **Cassini: a chief of staff.** It doesn't do the work. It makes sure the other five do: it follows up, checks their quality, chases what's stuck and reports to you in one line.
32. **Juno: a second opinion.** It reviews anything before it goes out (your writing, code, a plan, a purchase) and tells you what's wrong and how to fix it.
33. **Ceres: a librarian.** It keeps the Galaxy's knowledge clean: merges duplicate memories, retires outdated ones, and asks when two disagree.
34. **Hermes: a messenger.** It handles every back-and-forth for you (scheduling, confirming, chasing replies) across Messages, Mail and Discord, drafting each reply for your thumbs up.
35. **Athena: a strategist.** For anything with options, it lays out the choices, the trade-offs and a recommendation, then hands the chosen plan to Zenith.
36. **Pandora: an inventor.** It builds small tools the Galaxy needs on the spot (scripts, shortcuts, mini apps) and gives them to the other members.
37. **Mercury: speed itself.** It anticipates what you'll ask next and has it ready the moment you ask.
38. **Gemini: two of you.** It handles two things at once when you're busy: one on the Mac, one in the cloud, coordinated.
39. **Aegis: a gatekeeper.** Every action any member takes that matters passes through it, checked against your rules. You set the rules once, in plain English.
40. **Nyx: a night shift.** While you sleep it tidies, organises, updates, backs up and prepares, and has a short summary waiting when you open the Mac.

## E. Bigger swings
Each of these changes what the whole Galaxy is.

41. **Cosmos: the Galaxy as one app.** One window where every member lives together, and you never think about which one to ask.
42. **Andromeda: a Galaxy for others.** Spin up a Galaxy for a family member or friend, with your permission, sharing nothing private.
43. **Starlink: the Galaxy talks to other AIs.** It uses ChatGPT, Gemini or any other AI as a tool when one is better at something, while keeping your memory in the Galaxy.
44. **Genesis: the Galaxy builds its own members.** Describe a new member and it builds, tests and adds it, with an intro in Galaxy Chat.
45. **Eclipse: privacy mode.** Everything runs locally and nothing leaves the Mac, at the flip of a switch.
46. **Apollo: the Galaxy goes portable.** A tiny dedicated device (an old iPhone in a stand) that is only the Galaxy, always ready.
47. **Horizon: a long-term memory of your life.** It remembers the important things across years (people, places, milestones, decisions) and brings them back when they matter.
48. **Meridian: the Galaxy's own time.** One shared calendar of everything every member is doing, has done and will do, so you see the Galaxy's whole week at a glance.
49. **Saturn: rings of permission.** Every member gets clearly drawn rings of what it may do, and you can see and adjust them in one place.
50. **Zodiac: personalities.** The members adapt their tone, detail and style to the moment (quick and short at 7am, thorough at 9pm), learned from you.

## The ten strongest
1. **Titan (#11), AI at scale.** Nobody's AI does a thousand things at once well, and everyone has piles of things to process.
2. **Prism (#12), turn anything into anything.** The most general verb there is.
3. **Meteor (#4), one key for the next step.** Zero prompting, in every app, all day.
4. **Io (#3), speak anywhere it should write.** Everyone writes all day, in every app.
5. **Relay (#19), between apps.** Ends copy and paste between apps.
6. **Halo (#1), presence on every device.** The Galaxy stops being stuck on one device.
7. **Lumen (#6), lives in the notch.** Always there, never in the way.
8. **Pulsar (#21), hearing.** A new sense, with transcription that's free and runs on the Mac.
9. **Shadow (#14), learns by watching.** It automates you without you explaining anything.
10. **Mirage (#16), a dry run of anything.** The confidence layer every action needs.
