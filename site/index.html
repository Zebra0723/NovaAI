How it works

One hub. Many hands.

01

Galaxy Link is the hub

Every member checks in with Galaxy Link on your Mac. It hands work from one member to another, keeps one shared memory, and holds the questions that need your yes. A task you give Orbit by voice can be done by Nova on screen and finished by Zenith overnight.

02

Private by design

Galaxy runs on your Mac. It thinks with your own Claude account, and your memories belong to your Galaxy account and nobody else's. Nothing you say to one person's Galaxy is ever seen by another's.

03

Better every night

While you sleep, the Trainer runs each member through realistic drills aimed at its weak spots, grades them and rewrites the playbook it reads before every job. Medic checks on everyone once a minute and restarts anything that falls over.

04

You stay in charge

Anything that spends money, can't be undone or goes public waits for your yes, from the Link app, a thumbs up to Nebula, or out loud to Orbit. Orbit never speaks unless you speak first.
